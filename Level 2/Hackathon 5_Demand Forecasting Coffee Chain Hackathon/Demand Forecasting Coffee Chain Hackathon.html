~4,400 stores · two operational decisions every day

The forecast drives two critical workflows.

Workflow 01

Inventory optimization

How much of each category does each store need today? Understocking loses revenue. Overstocking creates waste.

Workflow 02

Order optimization

Inventory feeds supplier ordering. Forecasts at the right horizon let stores order at the right cadence and quantity.

06Gotcha №1
Why we forecastThe taskData & rulesSubmission

Stand on the decision day, not the forecast day

Every feature shifts by ≥ h days.

Decision day = the day you run the model and generate predictions  ·  Forecast date = the future day you are predicting sales for.

Leakage check

lag_1 in a 7d model = leak.

A 7-day rolling mean of last week's sales in a 30d model = leak. Last week is inside the forbidden window. The window must start at least 30 days back. Judges will check this in code review.

10The target
Why we forecastThe taskData & rulesSubmission

TXN ⨝ ORDER ⨝ PRODUCT: sum units_sold

Build the target from transactions.

# Canonical target for any (store_id, category, date)
target = (TRANSACTION
          .merge(ORDER[["order_id","store_id","date"]], on="order_id")
          .merge(PRODUCT[["product_id","category"]], on="product_id")
          .groupby(["store_id","category","date"])["units_sold"]
          .sum())
Zero days count. Days with no sales = 0 in the target. Submission must include every (store, category, date, h); no missing rows.

11Heads-up
Why we forecastThe taskData & rulesSubmission

Things you'll trip over if you don't read first

Five data integrity gotchas.

  • discount_applied is a percent (0–100), not a fraction. Verify: revenue ≈ base_price × units × (1 − discount/100)
  • INVENTORY.units_sold is noisy: only ~12% match TXN at SKU/day grain. Use INVENTORY for is_stockout and stock state, not for the target.
  • PROMOTION, LOCAL_EVENT, DATE_DIM: train and test versions are byte-identical lookups.
  • ~60% of orders have customer_id = null (walk-ins). Structural, not missing data.
  • Test stockout rate is 8.20% vs train 6.58%: slight drift; plan for it if your model is stockout-aware.

12Stockouts
Why we forecastThe taskData & rulesSubmission

Predict the recorded value: same definition as training

Don't try to uncensor demand.

  • On stockout days, the recorded value is the truncated quantity (sales stop when stock = 0).
  • You do not need to recover lost demand; the grader compares your predictions to the recorded historical values.
  • Predict the same target the training data shows.
Tip

Down-weight stockouts.

Try sample_weight ≈ 0.3 on stockout rows so the model isn't pulled toward truncated values.

13Metric
Why we forecastThe taskData & rulesSubmission

Lower is better · diagnostics ignored

One metric: MAE.

MAE = (1 / N) · Σ |ŷ − y|
  • Predictions must be non-negative; negatives are clipped to 0.
  • Evaluated across all (store × category × forecast_date × horizon) rows.
  • RMSE, R², MAPE may be reported as diagnostics; only MAE counts for ranking.
